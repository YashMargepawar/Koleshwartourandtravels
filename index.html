Questions

Questions

How many people can travel?

We have 8, 13, 17, 20, 26, 32 and 50 seater vehicles, and an Ertiga. Tell us your group size and we suggest the right one.

What are the rates?

For the 20 seater: ₹24 per km non-AC and ₹28 per km AC. For other vehicles, call or WhatsApp for rates. The final quote depends on your route, number of days and season.

What is inside the vehicle?

Comfortable seats, AC, charging points, a sound system, curtains and interior lights.

Where do you go?

Anywhere in India, with Maharashtra as our home ground. We do not run international trips.

How do I book?

Send the enquiry form (it opens WhatsApp) or call 7744073607. We share the quote, you confirm, and we plan the trip.

Can you suggest a route?

Yes. Tell us how many days you have and what kind of trip you want (family, pilgrimage, hills, beaches) and we will suggest a route.
